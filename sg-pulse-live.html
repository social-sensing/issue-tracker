About the dashboard
Reception and sentiment are assessed from publicly available data only (news media and public social posts/comments) — not private channels. The official↔public and official↔media “gap” figures are a qualitative read, not a precise measurement; sentiment is inferred and should guide attention, not decisions on its own. Figures cover only the selected time range and the issues currently tracked; an issue needs at least two of official / media / public to appear. Data is captured on a scheduled crawl, so recent items may not yet appear and a short delay is expected; tap Refresh for the latest stored data. Counts can change as new items are captured, de-duplicated and clustered. Personal identifiers are redacted. This is an internal sensing tool for situational awareness.
